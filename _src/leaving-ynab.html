Leaving YNAB?

You already know how to give every dollar a job. Stash lets you keep doing that, with a budget you can watch move, for less than a tenth of the price.

Bring your budget with you

Your accounts, envelopes and history come over, and every balance matches what YNAB shows for this month. It takes about a minute.

1. Export from YNABOn a computer, or in Safari with Request Desktop Website, open YNAB. Click your plan’s name and choose Export Plan.
2. Open StashTap Coming from YNAB? Bring it over on the welcome screen, or Bring over from YNAB in You.
3. Pick the zipCheck the summary and tap Bring it over. The file is read on your iPhone and never leaves it.

What stays the same

  • Ready to AssignReady to assign
  • CategoriesEnvelopes
  • Credit Card PaymentsEach card’s bill envelope
  • TargetsGoals
  • Covering overspendingCover it

Spend on a card and the money moves from that envelope to the card’s bill, the way you’re used to.

What’s different

When pay comes in, you watch it land in Ready to assign and move into rent, groceries and the trip. When you spend, you see which envelope it came from. If the method never quite clicked, seeing it usually helps.

There’s no account to make and no bank to log in to. Your budget lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We don’t run servers that hold it, and we can’t see it. No ads.

Typing everything in is the slow part, so you can share a screenshot of your bank app and the spending fills in, or tell Siri what you spent.

What’s missing, honestly

  • No bank sync. That’s on purpose. You add spending yourself, from a screenshot or with Siri.
  • No shared budget yet. With a family plan, each person in your family gets Plus on their own budget. Sharing one budget with a partner is the next big thing we’re working on.
  • iPhone only. No iPad, Mac, web or Android app for now.
  • Tracking accounts stay behind. Stash only has accounts you budget from.
  • Targets don’t come over, because YNAB’s export doesn’t include them. Stash fills in each envelope’s usual amount so you can set goals in one pass.
