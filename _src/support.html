Support

Something not working, or a question about Stash? Email SUPPORT_EMAIL and we’ll get back to you. You can also tap Send feedback in Stash’s Help screen.

How do I sync between my devices?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on each device. Stash syncs on its own. In Stash, open You to check that it says Syncing with iCloud.

What is Ready to assign?

It’s money that doesn’t have a home yet. When you add income, it lands there. Assign it to your envelopes until Ready to assign reads $0.

I went over in an envelope. What now?

Tap the envelope and choose Cover it to move money from an envelope that has extra. If you leave it, it comes out of next month.

How do credit cards work?

Each card has its own envelope for the bill. When you spend on the card, that money moves from the spending envelope to the card’s envelope, ready for when you pay the bill.

How do I restore Stash Plus?

Open Stash Plus in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. With a family plan, each person in your Apple family gets Plus through Family Sharing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Stash.

How do I export or erase my budget?

Open You. Tap Export to CSV to save a copy, or Start over to erase everything on your phone and in iCloud. See the privacy policy for more.
