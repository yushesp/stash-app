See your money move

Stash is an envelope budget for iPhone. It logs your spending from your bank’s alerts, and it shows the year you could retire.

Download on the App Store

Stash: See Your Money Move is a zero-based envelope budget for iPhone. Every dollar you earn goes into an envelope, like Rent, Groceries or Savings, until Ready to assign reads $0. When you spend, Stash takes it out of the right envelope, so you always know what’s left. It works without a bank login: Stash reads the alerts your bank already sends you. It also estimates the year you could retire from a few answers about your age, pay and savings. The whole budget is free. Stash Plus adds auto-logging, iCloud sync, Siri and charts.

Spending logs itself

Turn on Auto-log once and your bank does the typing. When your bank’s app sends a purchase alert, an iPhone Shortcut hands it to Stash, which reads it on your phone and logs it to the right envelope: “Logged $14.20 at Taco Bell to Eating out.” It works with any bank or card that sends alerts, in your currency. On iOS versions before 27, Stash logs your Apple Pay taps instead. Nothing goes to a server, and Stash never asks for your bank password.

The year you could retire

Answer a few questions about your age, pay, accounts, rent and bills, and in about three minutes Stash shows the year you could retire and the number you need to get there. Change how much you save and watch the year move. A daily check-in keeps it current.

How the envelopes work

Money comes inAdd a paycheck and it lands in Ready to assign.
Give it a homeAssign it to envelopes like Rent, Groceries and Gas until Ready to assign reads $0.
Spend from itEach spend comes out of its envelope, so you always know what’s left.

Spend on a credit card and the money moves from that envelope to the card’s bill, so it’s there when the bill is due. Went over? Cover it from an envelope with extra.

Your money stays yours

No account to make and no bank login. Your budget lives on your iPhone, and with Plus, in your own iCloud. We don’t run servers that hold it, and we can’t see it. No ads. Anonymous usage counts can be turned off in Settings.
