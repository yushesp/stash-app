Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Stash is an envelope budget for iPhone. In short: your budget stays on your phone and in your own iCloud. We don’t run servers that store it, we can’t see it, we don’t show ads, we don’t track you, and we don’t sell anything about you.

Where your budget lives

Everything you put in Stash, like your envelopes, accounts, spends, payees, notes and goals, is stored on your iPhone. If you’re signed in to iCloud, Stash also keeps a copy in your private iCloud database so it syncs across your devices. That copy belongs to your iCloud account. Apple stores it under the Apple Privacy Policy, and we have no way to read it.

What we collect

Nothing from inside the app. There’s no account to create, no analytics, and no tracking. We don’t collect your contacts, location, photos or anything else from your phone.

  • Purchases. Stash Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells Stash on your phone what you’ve bought. We never see your card or Apple ID.
  • Email you send us. If you email us, we get your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply.
  • Crash reports. If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage numbers. You can turn this off in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

Screenshot import

When you import a screenshot of your bank or card activity, Stash reads it on your iPhone, using Apple’s on-device text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence on your device. The picture isn’t uploaded anywhere. Only the lines you choose to keep are saved to your budget.

Siri and Shortcuts

In Stash, under Siri & Shortcuts, you choose what Siri and Shortcuts may do, like reading your balances or logging a spend. Requests you make through Siri are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

In Stash, open You and tap Start over to erase your budget from your phone and from iCloud. You can also delete the app, and remove its iCloud data in your iPhone’s Settings: tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage. If you’ve emailed us and want that email deleted, just ask.

You can export your budget any time from You, with Export to CSV.

Children

Stash isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete your data. Since your budget stays with you, you can do most of this in the app. For anything else, email us and we’ll help, usually within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change matters, we’ll say so in the app.
