Every dollar needs a home

Stash is a simple envelope budget for iPhone. When money comes in, you give each dollar a job. When you spend, you see which envelope it came from.

How it works

Money comes inAdd a paycheck and it lands in Ready to assign.
Give it a homeAssign it to envelopes like Rent, Groceries and Gas until Ready to assign reads $0.
Spend from itLog a spend and it comes out of its envelope, so you always know what’s left.

Cards, done right

Spend on a credit card and the money moves from that envelope to the card’s bill, so it’s there when the bill is due.

Your money stays yours

Your budget lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We don’t run servers that hold it, and we can’t see it. No ads, no tracking.
